- **Year:** 2026
- **Journal:** Personality Disorders: Theory, Research, and Treatment (APA)
- **DOI:** 10.1037/per0000790
- **Google Scholar URL:** https://psycnet.apa.org/record/2028-47696-001 (PMID 42956204)

**Abstract:**

Paraphrased from the PubMed record (verbatim reproduction restricted): the study compared dispositional mindfulness and mentalizing across 153 adults (51 per group): individuals with BPD, psychiatric controls, and healthy controls. The BPD group showed markedly lower mindfulness than both comparison groups. Self-reported mentalizing differed less, with BPD participants scoring lower only relative to healthy controls. Observer-rated mentalizing showed no overall group effect. Mindfulness correlated with self-reported but not observer-rated mentalizing.
